# RD116 — Genuine review collection and current-data requirements

Tawfik's clarification, 23 October 2026. Prepared for Antigravity; not submitted to its conversation because computer control was unavailable.

The operational dashboard must show actual internet-sourced customer reviews for each Balubaid company and platform, with current collection status. A sample interface alone does not meet the requested outcome.

1. Independently locate and verify the official company accounts, websites and branch listings. Separate AutoZone, APICO, Balubaid Spare Parts and the other registered businesses. Do not ask the user to supply material that can be discovered publicly; identify genuine access/authorization gates precisely when necessary.
2. Implement actual permitted collection from supported sources, starting with an end-to-end working source. Show positive and negative reviews by company and platform. Keep sample records separate from verified mode and all executive findings.
3. Preserve the original text, exact source reference, company/branch identity, publication date, retrieval timestamp, rating where present and review/verification state as permitted by the source. A profile homepage is not evidence for an individual quotation. Retrieved feedback remains a customer's statement, not proof that its allegation is true.
4. Show last successful collection, last attempted collection, source coverage, failures, observed historical range and available versus unsupported capabilities. Use webhooks/streams only where actually supported and authorized; otherwise poll at an explicit permitted interval and label the freshness honestly. Do not label periodic polling as instantaneous real-time coverage.
5. Update the displayed data after successful ingestion, without fabricating fresh timestamps, counts, views, engagement or success status. On failure retain the last valid data with a stale/error marker. No accessible records is distinct from no customer problems.
6. For the requested three-year history, report actual per-source coverage. Google Places review samples are not a full historical corpus. Confirm access and permitted retrieval/analysis/storage/derivative/export rights before processing. Public visibility does not itself grant complete automated access. If access is unavailable, show Not connected/Unavailable and the specific resolution needed; do not bypass controls or activate paid services without authorization.
7. Prove the first genuine collection with successful run evidence, exact record references and reconciliation from stored eligible records to dashboard totals. Tests passing on synthetic inputs are not proof of live collection or complete correctness.
8. Address the review findings: prevent contradictory classification from silently counting the same review as both positive and negative; distinguish conflicting star/text signals. Compute totals over the full eligible set, not a top-10/top-100 display slice. Prioritize human-reviewed customer impact separately from low stars. Leave action owners and due dates unassigned until a human supplies/confirms them.
9. Deliver actual implementation, relevant checks, remaining access gates and collection freshness. Do not claim full-web coverage, production readiness, approved board reporting or statistically validated model accuracy without evidence. Do not send external messages, publish, deploy or purchase services under this clarification alone.

Current review baseline: company/platform review UI exists and selected regression tests pass, but branch identifiers are placeholders and no genuine review dataset was present at the last inspection. This document does not establish that a new collection has occurred.
